## Technical Specifications Implemented

### SPX CFD Trading Strategy
```yaml
Trigger Logic: 
  - LONG entry: Price rises ≥2 basis points from opening price
  - SHORT entry: Price drops ≤-2 basis points from opening price
  
Opening Price Reset:
  - Daily: Reset to first open price at 9:30 AM
  - After Trades: Reset to exit price after each trade closure

Market Hours:
  - Entry Window: 9:30 AM - 3:30 PM EST
  - Force Close: 4:00 PM EST (no exceptions)
  - Data May Continue: Until 4:05 PM for validation

Risk Management:
  - Account Risk: 2% per trade (configurable)
  - Stop Loss Distance: $50 default (configurable)
  - Risk/Reward Ratio: 2:1 (configurable)
  - Transaction Cost: $5 per round trip
```
