Long Trade (Buy) 📈

Risk & Position Sizing

Risk per Trade ($)ℹ️
Formula: (Account Balance) * (Account Risk %)
Risk per Contract ($)ℹ️
Formula: (Opening Price) - (Initial Stop-Loss Price)
Position Size (CFDs)ℹ️
Formula: (Risk per Trade $) / (Risk per Contract $)

Trade Setup

Leverage Ratioℹ️
Formula: 1 / (Margin Rate %)
Notional Value ($)ℹ️
Formula: (Position Size) * (Opening Price)
Margin Required ($)ℹ️
Formula: (Notional Value) * (Margin Rate %)

Scenario Analysis

Profit Target Price ($)ℹ️
Formula: (Opening Price) + ((Risk per Contract $) * (Risk/Reward Ratio))
Active Trailing Stop ($)ℹ️
A dynamic stop-loss that moves up as the price rises.
Final Exit Price ($)ℹ️
The calculated price where the trade would close based on current conditions.

Final Results

Gross Profit/Loss ($)ℹ️
Formula: ((Final Exit Price) - (Opening Price)) * (Position Size)
Net Profit ($)ℹ️
Formula: (Gross P/L) - (Cost * Position Size)
Return on Margin (%)ℹ️
Formula: (Net Profit) / (Margin Required)
